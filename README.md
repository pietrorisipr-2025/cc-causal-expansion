# CC_CAUSAL_EXPANSION

**Open exploratory research record on causal-light kinematics, causal sets, and quantum sequential growth**

**Research direction and conceptual origin:** Pietro Risi  
**Status:** Exploratory; **not peer-reviewed**; not an established physical theory.  
**Assistance:** Extensive use of OpenAI ChatGPT for mathematical derivations, software, numerical checks, figure generation and drafting; Anthropic Claude for an AI-based technical critique and reference checking. These are **not** human peer reviews.

> **Central result:** The initial “c + c” motivation has an interpretation in standard FLRW kinematics, but the project has **not derived** a new autonomous cosmological expansion law, a gravitational quantum measure, or a validated prediction for $H(z)$.

## What the project studied

1. **Light and FLRW horizons.** Kinematic identities for null rays, proper separations and cosmological horizon rates. These are not new dynamics.
2. **Thermodynamic / holographic closures.** Comparisons to established thermodynamic and dark-energy models; no independently selected $H(t)$.
3. **Causal-set statistical and quantum models.** Finite-size calculations and conditional results, with explicit limits on extrapolation to 4D gravitational physics.
4. **Quantum sequential growth and Bell causality.** Algebraic restrictions under a **strong operator formulation**. The initial exceptional XL family was later found to violate more general necessary relations; see [v1.03 corrigendum](audits/v1.03/R12BB_ER_DERIVATION_AND_LIMITS.md).
5. **Vector-vs-operator constraints.** Elementary finite counterexamples distinguish relations on one initial state from full operator identities; these are not solutions of quantum Bell causality.

## Scientific and editorial limitations

- This repository is **not** evidence of independent human verification of the AI-assisted proofs or of a peer-reviewed scientific result.
- Do not cite the old v0.92 classification as a globally admissible family; its claimed global survival was withdrawn in v1.03.
- The strong $2\times2$ noncommutative Hermitian obstruction **does not exclude every** $2\times2$ representation, non-Hermitian growth, state-level formulations, or higher dimensions.
- Historical numerical experiments have incomplete portability from the light recovery alone. Do not mark them independently reproduced without running the corresponding original code.
- The English overview (updated from the AI-assisted bibliography audit) is a selective synthesis of a substantially AI-assisted research notebook, not a full translation.
