# Provenance, authorship, and assistance

**Pietro Risi:** conceptual origin and research direction, questions, iterative selection of exploratory paths, review and decisions in the research workflow. This does **not** imply independent human rederivation of every mathematical result or human peer review.

**OpenAI ChatGPT:** substantial contributions to mathematical derivations, numerical/analytical calculations, coding, figures, report drafting and audit/recovery packaging.

**Anthropic Claude:** AI-generated technical referee-style review and bibliographic/prior-art check; independent in the sense of a different AI model and implementation, **not** independent human peer review.

Source records include original historical PASS, FAIL and INCONCLUSIVE outcomes. Automation checked some formulas but does not establish the physical interpretation, validity of all historical proofs or novelty in the literature.

The project includes existing scientific publications from other authors only as references, not as claimed project discoveries. Names of AI tools are disclosures of assistance, not authorship metadata.

If a qualified human expert evaluates any claim later, document the **specific** work done and obtain permission before listing them as reviewers or contributors.
