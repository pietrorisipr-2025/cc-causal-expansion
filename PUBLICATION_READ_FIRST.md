# Public release reading order and important limitations

**Start with the short English report v0.2**, then read the full cumulative
working paper v1.03 (374 pages, primarily Italian) together with the
**v1.03 XL--IR corrigendum**. For reproduction, consult the original
canonical v1.03 recovery distributed via a GitHub Release.

The cumulative notebook is a historic **research log**: old ideas and old
claims are intentionally not silently removed. In particular, the historical
v0.92 statement that the XL family survives all necessary operator QSG
constraints was **withdrawn** by the v1.03 corrigendum. Its restricted
algebraic calculations survive with narrowed scope.

No autonomous cosmological evolution law H(z), independently established
quantum measure, physical gravitational sourcing, or controlled 4D limit was
derived. Neither ChatGPT nor Claude supplied human peer review. Readers must
not treat automated arithmetic checks as expert validation.

The repository shares original materials openly under CC BY 4.0 for original
copyrightable documentation/figures and MIT for original software, only where
rights to license exist. Historical recovery data, external AI referee material
and third-party assets are not automatically relicensed wholesale.
