# Contributing and scientific corrections

This is an exploratory, AI-assisted open research notebook. We welcome **error reports, independent replications, bibliographic corrections, counterexamples and new physically motivated hypotheses**.

Please open a GitHub Issue for a small question or a pull request for a complete, reproducible change. Use the templates under `.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/` when applicable.

## Requirements

- Identify the exact document version, file path, page, equation and affected claim.
- State all assumptions and the formal domain (e.g., operator identities vs state equalities; Hermitian vs general $2\times2$).
- Supply a proof, counterexample or reproducible implementation. Distinguish symbolic proof from finite numerical checks.
- For computations, list dependencies, interpreter/version, data/source, random seeds, sample sizes and known limitations.
- Treat literature and AI-generated explanations as **inputs to check, not authority**. Cite first-party papers where possible.
- Preserve historical mistakes and their corrections through dated errata; do not silently replace old results.
- Do not fit new parameters merely to avoid previously documented negative findings.
- Clearly disclose use of AI in contributed content and take responsibility for verification.
- Confirm that you have the right to submit each contribution and that it may be distributed under the repository's applicable outbound license: CC BY 4.0 for original text/figures, MIT for original code. Identify exclusions and third-party licenses clearly. No contributor is required to publish future independent adaptations or research.

A merged pull request is **not** a scientific endorsement, a published peer review, or a certification of correctness.
