# Open questions and useful follow-up directions

Contributors may help with any of the following **without trying to rescue an ansatz by tuning parameters after seeing outcomes**.

1. **Axioms of quantum sequential growth.** Compare operator-valued Markov sum/general covariance assumptions with conditions asserted only on state vectors or decoherence functionals. Clarify what is physically required and which identities are additional assumptions.
2. **Bell partner independence.** Re-derive the source's general necessary conditions for arbitrary relevant causal-set transitions and compare atomization/partner choices; state every domain restriction.
3. **Unaddressed representation classes.** Analyze explicitly the branch $Q_2=\lambda Q_1$ with potentially noncommuting $Q_3$; also non-Hermitian, singular and higher-dimensional operator families. No existing calculation excludes them all.
4. **2D causal-set fixed-cardinality identities.** Establish complete mathematical proofs and an exhaustive specialist prior-art comparison for R12y–R12ac, distinguishing fixed-$N$ ensembles from Poisson ensembles.
5. **Independent replay of historical numerical gates.** Locate and publish source dependencies from earlier archived ZIPs, with hashes, run instructions, and any null/failed outcomes.
6. **A physically derived 4D theory.** Any proposal should specify sample space, quantum measure/state, globally consistent dynamics, an on-shell conserved source/response, a controlled 3+1D limit, and independent predictions. These are currently missing.

## Desired evidence from contributors

Use a link to a reference and exact equation number; add scripts with dependencies, deterministic seeds where relevant, independent test cases and negative controls; identify whether a claim is **proved**, **empirically observed**, **inconclusive**, or **not tested**. Keep failed or null findings.
