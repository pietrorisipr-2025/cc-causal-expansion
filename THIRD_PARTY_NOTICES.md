This repository is an open, AI-assisted exploratory research archive. Its
original textual and graphical content and software are made available under
the licenses described in [LICENSE](LICENSE), only where licensing rights exist.

## Exclusions and review

- **Academic citations** in the BibTeX/LaTeX/PDF are references to papers by
  their respective authors. Neither those papers nor their externally supplied
  figures, excerpts or software become CC BY 4.0/MIT material just because they
  are cited. The source links and publication details should be retained.
- **External referee output:** `audits/AI_REFEREE_REPORT_v1.02.md` contains a
  report supplied through Anthropic Claude. It remains attributed to that
  tool and the research workflow; the repository does not grant a blanket
  sublicense over rights, if any, belonging to other parties. The internal
  triage is likewise provenance evidence, not independent human peer review.
- **Historical recovery and earlier ZIPs:** an original snapshot may contain
  independently attributed files or external content. The archive is preserved
  unmodified, and it is not automatically sublicensed as a whole by the
  repository-level notices. Verify individual rights and notices before reuse.
- **AI-produced text, code and figures:** copyright status and ownership may
  vary with applicable law and source terms. Permission under LICENSE is only
  given to the extent the licensor has rights to grant; no claim is made that
  purely machine-produced material necessarily carries copyright.
- **Runtime dependencies:** imported third-party Python packages retain their
  own applicable licenses; they are not bundled or relicensed by the script's
  MIT notice.

## For contributors and reusers

Before distributing or adapting content, verify the origin and applicable
license of the specific files. Flag questionable ownership or unattributed
material via an issue. License notices and attribution to the original human
research direction should be preserved where required.
